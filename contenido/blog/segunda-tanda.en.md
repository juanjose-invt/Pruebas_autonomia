The notebook had four projects and now it has eight. The four new ones are on the projects page, each with its own sketch and in its own folder.

## What was added

- **Quotes**: a free generator of quotes as PDF files. It is the way in to [Ofiza](https://ofiza.es), a SaaS for the self-employed.
- **Dashboard**: an instant data dashboard from a CSV or an Excel file. The data never leaves the device, and the look is risograph.
- **Scanner**: a scanner for receipts and documents with text recognition on the device itself, with a scanner-bed look.
- **Pizza**: a pizza dough guide with a calculator, configurable recipes and a 3D step-by-step, with a Neapolitan pizzeria look.

> [margin] Each app has its own style and its own typeface. The only thing they share is the «by innovativecode» signature in the footer.

## How it fits into the site

The build now copies eight folders into `_site/` instead of four. If one does not exist yet, it skips it and says so at the end, without failing. In the portfolio, the front page shows all eight sketches and the projects page sets the second batch apart from the first.
